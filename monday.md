## 1. Problem statement  (2–3 specific sentences)
What is the problem · who has it · why it matters.
> Buyers can't tell whether a listed item's actual condition matches its photos and description, which makes them hesitant to contact the seller and pushes them toward other platforms.

## 2. Primary persona
- Snapshot (name + one-line situation): Sonia, 30, is furnishing her new apartment on a tight budget.
- Context (when / where they hit this — device, moment, how often): She browses ReNest a few times a week on her phone at night, hoping to find an affordable sofa within a ~20-minute drive before she finishes moving in next month.
- Goals (1–2, what they're trying to get done): Find a decent sofa close to her location.
- Pains (1–3, specific to this problem): She can't verify an item's actual condition from photos and descriptions alone, and has arrived at pickups to find items visibly more worn or damaged than pictured — wasting a trip and eroding her trust in listings.
- Current workaround (how they deal with it today): She looks at alternatives like Facebook Marketplace, or buys new furniture to avoid the risk.
- The job (JTBD): "When I'm buying furniture on a tight budget, I want to know a product's real condition before messaging the seller, so I can save time and find the items I actually want."
- In their words (a short quote): "Photos always look good, but the product is almost never as shown."

## 3. North Star Metric
- North Star: Weekly completed purchases with no condition-related dispute or return — a buyer contacts a seller, buys the item, and the transaction closes without the buyer flagging that the item didn't match its photos or description.
- Why it reflects real user value (not a vanity number): A purchase that closes cleanly, with no condition dispute or return, is direct behavioral evidence the buyer trusted the listing enough to act and got what was promised. It doesn't depend on a separate, voluntary step like leaving a review, so it won't undercount cases where the trust problem was actually solved — and the weekly cadence makes it trackable as a rate the team can move.

## 4. Value proposition  (pain → gain)
What changes for the persona when this is solved.
> For buyers on a tight budget who can't judge a used item's real condition from a listing, ReNest surfaces enough trustworthy signal — clear condition details, real photos, responsive sellers — that they can reach out with confidence. Less time wasted on misrepresented items, more good sofas found nearby.
